# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Formidable is a technical project maintained in good faith. Everyone who participates — whether reporting a bug, opening a PR, or joining a discussion — deserves a straightforward, respectful experience.

## What We Expect

**Be direct and constructive.**
Disagree with ideas, not people. If something is wrong, say what's wrong and why. If you have a better approach, propose it.

**Be patient.**
This is an open source project maintained in spare time. Responses may take days. Incomplete PRs happen. That's normal.

**Stay on topic.**
Issues and discussions are for the library. Keep conversations relevant.

**Assume good intent.**
Most misunderstandings come from tone, not malice. Before escalating, assume the other person meant well.

## What We Won't Tolerate

- Personal attacks or insults
- Harassment of any kind
- Dismissing contributors based on experience level
- Spam, self-promotion, or off-topic noise

## Enforcement

Violations may result in comments being removed, issues being closed, or contributors being blocked — at the maintainer's discretion. There is no formal appeals process for minor incidents.

For serious concerns, contact the maintainer directly via GitHub.

---

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other forum tied to this repository.
